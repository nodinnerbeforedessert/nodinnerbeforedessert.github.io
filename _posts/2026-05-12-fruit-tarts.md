I made a handful of fruit tarts (but failed to take a picture) so just need to save the links here.<br>

Blackberry - which I've done before from [this recipe](https://www.simplyrecipes.com/recipes/blackberry_pie/)<br>

Red Currant<br>
Filled the tart shell with [red currant filling](https://redcurrantbakery.com/red-currant-meringue-tart/) and topped with some meringue from that same recipe. <br>

Strawberry + (Pastry Cream)<br>
I used my [usual pastry cream recipe](https://preppykitchen.com/vanilla-custard/), topped with [my usual strawberry sauce](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/43914/supreme-strawberry-topping/). <br>

Strawberry Meringue<br>
A layer of [Strawberry Frosting](https://sugarspunrun.com/chocolate-covered-strawberry-cookies/#wprm-recipe-container-75161), topped with some meringe and then a spoon of strawberry sauce to show that it's strawberry.<br>

Raspberry Pie<br>
I kept this one simple and made a batch of this [pie filling](https://bake-eat-repeat.com/raspberry-pie-recipe/) baked separately and transferred into the tart shell.<br>
